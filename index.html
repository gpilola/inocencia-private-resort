Welcome to Inocencia

A private escape made for shared moments.

Located in Sitio Ibaba, Sto. Domingo, Bay, Laguna, Inocencia Private Resort & Events Place welcomes families, friends, and groups looking for a comfortable place to unwind or celebrate.

Enjoy a private pool experience, group-friendly accommodations, a functional kitchen, karaoke, billiards, free Wi-Fi, and an events area for meaningful occasions.
